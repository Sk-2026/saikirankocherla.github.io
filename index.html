About me

I am an AI researcher focused on building intelligent systems for autonomous vehicles, with an emphasis on real-time perception and control. My work involves designing models that can understand complex environments and make reliable decisions under dynamic conditions.

I also explore Generative AI, including Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs), to develop multimodal and adaptive AI systems. Previously, I worked on UAV-based hyperspectral imaging and remote sensing, applying machine learning and signal-processing techniques to analyze agricultural indicators such as crop health and water stress.

I am particularly interested in bridging research and real-world deployment, and I actively work on projects that lead to publications, practical systems, and scalable AI solutions.
